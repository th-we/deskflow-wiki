# NetMouse (Mac OS 7)

In 1992, at [MacHack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacHack), Eric Hayes partnered with a (future) co-worker to write what they called ''NetMouse'', a very rough predecessor to Synergy.

<blockquote>NetMouse, an even more useful utility, came from Jorg Brown and Eric Hayes, allows you to control another Mac on a network with the mouse and keyboard on your Mac. NetMouse would be wonderful for working with a PowerBook and main Mac, or for something like testing a multi-user database.</blockquote

The tool was one of the five Winning Hacks of that year's conference, as reported in [issue 135](http://tidbits.com/static/html/TidBITS-135.html#lnk3) of the [TidBITS newsletter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidbits) (July 1992), and in [volume 2, number 7](http://www.callapple.org/Members/magazine/co-op/CoopSpirit_1992-09.pdf) of the [Co-Op Spirit magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Pugetsound_Program_Library_Exchange#Publications) (September 1992).
