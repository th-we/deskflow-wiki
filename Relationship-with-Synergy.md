Deskflow is the upstream of Synergy. Deskflow is completely open source, and Synergy is mostly open source.
Whereas Deskflow is a community-driven project focused entirely on goals decided on by the developer community,
Synergy is adapted to fit customer needs with business and enterprise licensing available.
Synergy has a thorough QA process, warranty, and technical support.
Deskflow has a faster release schedule and focuses on bleeding edge changes.
The Deskflow project is sponsored by Synergy. [More info about Synergy](https://symless.com/synergy).
